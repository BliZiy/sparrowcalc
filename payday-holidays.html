What happens when payday falls on a holiday?

Direct deposits move through the ACH network, which doesn't settle payments on weekends or Federal Reserve holidays. When a payday lands on one of those days, most employers pay on the business day before; some pay the business day after.

Two rules people often miss: when a holiday falls on a Saturday, Federal Reserve Banks stay open the Friday before, so Friday deposits usually aren't affected. When a holiday falls on a Sunday, banks close the following Monday. In 2027 that means Monday, July 5 is closed, while Friday, June 18 and Friday, December 24 are normal banking days.
