Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026.

The short version

SparrowCalc calculations happen entirely in your browser. The numbers you type into a calculator, such as pay, bills, and debts, are never sent to us or stored by us. Close the tab and they're gone.

What we don't collect

  • We don't have accounts, sign-ups, or forms that send data to a server.
  • We don't receive or save any calculator inputs or results.
  • We don't currently use cookies, analytics, or advertising scripts on SparrowCalc. If that changes, we'll update this page first and describe what is used.

Hosting

SparrowCalc is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, for security and operations. That's covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement. Your calculator inputs are never part of those requests.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other sites, including the Sheetsparrow store on Payhip. Some links may be affiliate or partner links in the future, and they'll be labeled. When you click a link and leave SparrowCalc, the other site's privacy policy applies, and that site may set its own cookies. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

Children

SparrowCalc is a general-audience budgeting site and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Questions? See the contact page.

Please note: SparrowCalc is for personal budgeting only. It is not financial, tax, or legal advice, and results are estimates based on what you enter. Check important numbers with your lender, employer, or a qualified professional. Designed with the help of AI tools.
