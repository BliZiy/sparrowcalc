Why 3-paycheck months matter

Many biweekly budgeters plan each month around two paychecks. Then in the two months with a third check, that whole check is free: none of your regular bills depend on it. Common uses are an emergency fund, an extra debt payment, or sinking funds for car repairs and holidays.

In some years a biweekly schedule has 27 paydays. That happens when January 1 (or January 2 in a leap year) is a payday. Your employer's pay calendar always wins if it differs, for example when a payday moves because of a bank holiday.
