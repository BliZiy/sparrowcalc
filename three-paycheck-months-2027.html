Three-paycheck months in 2027

Paid every two weeks? Find your first payday of 2027 in the table below to see which months bring a third paycheck, and whether 2027 is a 27-paycheck year for you.

By SparrowCalc · Updated October 2026 · About 3 minutes

The quick answer for Friday paydays

If your first 2027 payday is Friday, January 1, your three-paycheck months are January, July and December and you get 27 paychecks. If your first 2027 payday is Friday, January 8, your three-paycheck months are April and October. Not sure which one is yours? Your 2026 schedule carries over: count forward two weeks from your last 2026 payday.
