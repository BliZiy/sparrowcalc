Budget by paycheck, not by month

Most budgeting advice assumes you get one big check on the first of the month. Real paychecks don't work like that. If you're paid semi-monthly (on the 1st and 15th), a rent payment due on the 3rd and a car payment due on the 12th both come out of the same check. If you're paid biweekly, you get 26 checks a year, so two months each year have three paydays. That extra check is easy to spend without noticing.

SparrowCalc's tools sort out the timing so you know which check pays which bill, how much is left over, and where extra money can go, such as paying down debt faster. Every calculation happens entirely in your browser, and the numbers you enter are never sent or stored. New here? Read our paycheck budgeting articles.
