Contact

Questions, bug reports, and calculator ideas are welcome.

The best way to reach us

Open an issue on our public GitHub repository:

Contact us via GitHub Issues →

You'll need a free GitHub account. Issues are public, so please don't include personal financial details, account numbers, or anything private.

Good things to send

  • A calculation that looks wrong. Tell us which tool, what you entered (use made-up numbers), and what you expected.
  • A page that doesn't display properly on your device or browser.
  • Ideas for new calculators or articles.

Questions about a Sheetsparrow spreadsheet you bought? Use the contact option on the Sheetsparrow store.
