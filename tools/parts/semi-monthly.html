How the split works

A check that arrives on the 1st has to cover everything due before the next check on the 15th. So bills due on days 1–14 go to the 1st paycheck and bills due on days 15–31 go to the 15th paycheck. If one check ends up short, use "Paid from" to move a flexible bill to the other check, or ask the company if you can change the due date.

Tip: if your employer pays early when the 1st or 15th falls on a weekend, plan for that check to arrive a day or two before the bills it covers.
