About SparrowCalc

Free, simple calculators for people who budget one paycheck at a time.

Why SparrowCalc exists

Most budgeting tools assume you get paid once a month. Many people don't. If you're paid on the 1st and 15th, or every other Friday, the hard part isn't the total. It's the timing: which check covers rent, what's left before the next payday, and what to do with the occasional extra check.

SparrowCalc is a small set of free calculators built for that problem. They need no sign-up and no download, and they work on a phone.

Who makes it

SparrowCalc is made by Sheetsparrow, an independent shop that sells budgeting spreadsheets for Excel and Google Sheets. The calculators are free to use. Some pages link to Sheetsparrow spreadsheets for people who want a planner they can keep and update all year. That's how the site is funded. Read the disclosure for details.

How the tools work

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser using plain JavaScript. The numbers you enter are never sent to us or stored. The privacy policy explains this in full.

The site was designed with the help of AI tools and reviewed by a person. The tools give estimates based on what you enter. They aren't financial, tax, or legal advice.
